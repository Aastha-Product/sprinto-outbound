Why it holds up in a review

A research partner that shows its work.

The language model reads and answers questions. Python checks the answers and does the scoring, so the same facts always give the same verdict.

Evidence-checked scoring

Each yes needs a quote, and the quote must really appear on a page we fetched. No quote, no points. The point values live in one editable file, the ICP.

An agent loop that digs

Borderline or missing facts trigger up to three research rounds: read another page on the site, search the web, or stop. Every round is logged with what it changed.

Guardrails on every send

The recipient is fixed to the owner's inbox, drafts are checked for length, links and banned phrases, and contact search only runs for companies that qualify.

The pipeline

From a bare domain to a reply-worthy first line.

Rejections happen early and cheaply: competitors, existing Sprinto customers and consumer brands are caught by fixed lists before any scoring call. Only qualified companies use contact credits.
